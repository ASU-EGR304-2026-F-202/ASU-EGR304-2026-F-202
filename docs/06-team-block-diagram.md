## Introduction

Our design idea was to have three separate sensor PCB's each transmit a through a UART connection to a LCD screen. 
## Research Question

* What communication type will be used?
* What ports will be used?
* What are the primary in PCB components involved?

## Conclusion

We will use UART due to the requirement of displaying actual numbers and team familiarity, three separate ribbon cables with UART communication for easier troubleshooting, and there will only be 1 way communication from sensors to LCD.
