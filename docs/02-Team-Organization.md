## Team Charter
From our initial team discussions, our team charter formed to be:
> "Our team is committed to collaboratively designing a high-quality embedded system through disciplined engineering practices, equal accountability, and transparent communication, ensuring every team member achieves core technical mastery and professional growth."
### Shared Team Goals

1. **Portfolio-Grade System Design:** Engineer a fully functional embedded product with clean hardware-software integration that serves as a standout engineering portfolio piece.
2. **Version Control Integrity:** Maintain an organized GitHub repository using structured branches, regular commits, and clear Markdown documentation.
3. **Firmware & Hardware Proficiency:** Develop practical mastery over low-level microcontroller programming, peripheral configuration, and circuit schematic capture.
4. **Schedule Discipline:** Complete and review all assignment deliverables at least 24 hours before class deadlines.
5. **Transparent Collaboration:** Foster an equitable team dynamic with open communication, equal workload distribution, and active peer support.

---
## Product Mission Statement

> "To design and construct a reliable, modular embedded system that delivers accurate real-time data processing and transparent hardware feedback for practical user applications."

**Mission Context:**  
This mission statement defines the distilled purpose of our physical device. Our goal is to bridge the gap between low-level hardware control and clear system status representation, prioritizing functional stability, modular design, and user accessibility.

## Team Coordination, Accountability & Conflict Resolution

* **Assignment Sign-off:** Every deliverable must be reviewed and approved by all team members before final submission.
* **Addressing Missed Commitments:** If a team member misses a task deadline, an informal check-in will occur within 24 hours to reassess workload distribution.
* **Under-performance Protocol:** Continued unexcused missed contributions will result in a formal team discussion, followed by instructor notification if performance does not improve.
* **Conflict Resolution:** Technical disagreements will be evaluated objectively against project goals; unresolved issues will be escalated to the instructor during office hours.
